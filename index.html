DAC

Simulated in LTspice and validated waveforms on an oscilloscope. Designed and printed a prototype PCB using Fusion 360, then soldered components with an iron and a reflow oven. The circuit included Arduino-driven binary inputs and a summing amplifier circuit to flip and "shrink" the signal.

  • Fusion 360
  • LTspice
  • Reflow oven
